1) Business Problem Summary

ElectraHub wants to understand what most influences product sales so the team can invest marketing budget better, choose stronger campaign mixes, and improve product presentation.

The goal is to predict sales and turn those findings into practical actions for the marketing and merchandising teams.

4) Recommendations for Marketing Team

  • Prioritize campaigns that raise engagement, because this is the strongest single driver in simple models.
  • Use ad budget increases carefully where engagement potential is high; budget alone helps, but less than engagement quality.
  • Expand influencer campaigns in segments where performance is strong (model shows about $917 lift vs email, all else equal).
  • Use this model for planning scenarios before launch (for example budget shifts, channel mix, and expected sales impact).

5) Recommendations for Merchandising Team

  • Protect and improve customer ratings: moving rating from 3.8 to 4.2 links to an estimated sales gain of about $9,629 in the simple model.
  • Strengthen content and product presentation for low-popularity items first to move them into higher popularity bands.
  • Plan inventory with campaign plans together, so top campaigns are fully supported when demand spikes.
  • Keep controlled discounting: discounts help, but should be combined with strong campaign engagement and product quality.

6) Known Model Limitations

  • The model learns from historical data only; future market changes can reduce accuracy.
  • Some predictors move together strongly, which can make individual coefficient values less stable even if total prediction quality is strong.
  • The model does not include outside factors such as seasonality, competitor promotions, or stockouts unless they are already captured in current columns.
  • Relationship strength does not prove cause-and-effect; recommendations should be validated with controlled tests when possible.
  • Popularity was converted into an ordered scale; if business meaning changes, this should be revisited.
