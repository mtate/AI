Credit Decision Support

Insights Report

This report summarizes the lending data, compares the simple benchmark rules and tree models, and explains what the winning tree is looking at. The goal is to support fair, consistent, and easy-to-review lending decisions.

Business Problem

The business question is whether we can help predict loan approval decisions in a way that is accurate, consistent, and easy to explain. The preferred model should support credit review, not replace it.

Recommendations for the Risk Committee

  • Use the post-pruned tree as the main decision support tool. It keeps the review simple and performs well on the holdout set.
  • Give additional review to applicants with borderline credit scores or high debt burdens, because those are the strongest drivers of the decision.
  • Avoid relying on gender in any decision rule. The model does not use it, and the observed group approval rates should be monitored separately for fairness.
  • Keep a human review path for unusual cases, especially applicants with prior default or bankruptcy history.

Known Limitations

  • The model is trained on one historical dataset, so it may not hold up as applicant behavior changes over time.
  • The tree is easier to explain than many other models, but it is still only a simplified view of a more complex lending decision.
  • The reported group-level checks are descriptive, not a full fairness audit.
